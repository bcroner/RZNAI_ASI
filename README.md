# RZNAI_ASI
"Artificial Superintelligence"- a 3SAT simulation of a prefrontal cortext that directs a-la-autopiloted AGIs according to an extended purpose, which may be a series of goals among changing motivational contexts.

These AGIs are dispatched on a continuous basis to collect small, contained factoids about the situation as it pertains to the overall stated goal. These factoids are in the form of deductive logic constructs that extend outward by just one extra step according to some sort of assigned context-free grammar that each of the AGIs is responsible for collecting. In an undergraduate computer science curriculum, this is known as a binary search, but we play with this term loosely here. If analyzing the surface of a sphere, we're looking at a reduction down to 3/4 distance down the sphere for each step, going in each of the positive and negative directions, one quarter turn each away from each other, forming 4 total tests. If otherwise not the surface of a curved geometric landscape, we're talking about either Eudlidean geometry or raw 3D mathematics and/or logic, in which case we may close the distance by 1/2 per iteration. Achieving overall satisfiability results in the reassignment of the positions of context (in atomic bonding, repositioning the placements of the bonds on different relative parts of the atoms, as in for predictive proteomics for protein folding, and for the cinematic experience using novel game engine technology, the amusement and continued engagement of the user, as has been previously collected by prior cinematic experiences for a further customization of the immersive cinematic world in which the goal is to keep the user engaged so that they may be shown more ads, for example). Failing to achieve satisfiability results in no change in the arrangement of the points of context, and the distances still reduce by 3/4 for curved surface geometries and 1/2 for Euclidean and 3D geometries and raw math and/or logic.

One important implementation detail is we use one's complement mathematics here. It is not obsolete, as claimed by computer science mathematics textbook authors. It is ideal for this purpose.
